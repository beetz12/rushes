# PCM audio rendering repair

The assembled native gate exposed an audio-reader-stalled error in simultaneous edited share exports. A controlled baseline of six reordered/speed-2 exports and 24 full package decodes passed in 1.913 seconds. Adding 24 separate four-track writer fixtures crashed twice, including after importing root's exact canonical pixel-allocation fixture fix. Owned helper PIDs 3009 and 9056 faulted inside FigSimpleMutexUnlock, called from FigSemaphoreWaitRelative and AVAssetReaderOutput.copyNextSampleBuffer on the audio read queue. The logs and reports are preserved in /Users/dave/.goal-loop/loom-clone/host/pcm-audio-pressure. This reproduces failure on the old compressed composition/mix-reader path; it does not establish a global platform resource limit.

NativeRenderer now consumes independently cached decoded PCM, maps clip order, offsets and microphone latency explicitly, sums gains in bounded Float32 blocks and writes a temporary mixed CAF. Speed changes use per-clip AVAudioUnitTimePitch at the requested rate and zero pitch shift. AVAudioEngine enters offline mode before accessing mixer/input/output nodes, as required by the installed Apple SDK contract. Both processing buffers are bounded at 4096 frames; partial offline renders are retained before requesting the remainder. Output uses the exact cumulatively rounded 48 kHz frame count of the edited timeline. No whole-recording PCM array is allocated.

An intermediate version passed the exact writer-pressure test but still used AVAssetReaderTrackOutput to feed the mixed CAF to the writer. The broader six-suite concurrent filter crashed after 32.970 seconds: helper PID 89268 faulted in WaitOnCondition/FigSemaphoreWaitRelative from that final track reader in NativeRenderer.AudioPump. Its report and extracted stack are saved alongside the earlier evidence. The final implementation removes that reader entirely. AudioPump reads the CAF through AVAudioFile, copies each bounded block into an owned CMSampleBuffer and supplies per-frame timing at 48 kHz. Audio and video writers remain independently driven and joined; failed or cancelled work removes its temporary mixed CAF. Original masters remain untouched. Native render cache version 3 prevents older exports from masking the new path.
